01 / Context

What was broken.

The starting state
Problem

The starting state.

  • Buyers spent hours per day chasing live lead times, pricing, and inventory across 4+ disconnected systems.
  • Critical procurement decisions made on stale spreadsheets — corrected days later when reality caught up.
  • ERP updates lagged real-world supplier conversations by 2-5 business days, distorting planning downstream.
Approach

What I built.

  • Indexed authoritative procurement data into a SharePoint-backed RAG knowledge base — single query surface for buyers and planners.
  • Built a human-in-the-loop approval workflow: AI drafts the ERP-ready update, a buyer approves or edits, the system commits.
  • Instrumented department-level adoption from day one — Engineering led, Manufacturing and Product followed.
  • Tight feedback loop: every HITL correction trained the next iteration of prompts and retrieval scoring.
