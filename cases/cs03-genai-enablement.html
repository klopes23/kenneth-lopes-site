01 / Context

What was broken.

The starting state
Problem

The starting state.

  • Access to GenAI tools did not guarantee meaningful organizational usage.
  • Teams lacked role-based context, structure, and habit-forming templates.
  • Pockets of power-users emerged organically; everyone else stayed on the sidelines — and leadership had no instrumentation to tell an adoption story.
Approach

What I built.

  • Operationalized an intake-to-execution workflow with zero-context training assets.
  • Built a role-based template library (Context · Format · Constraints) for manager · IC · analyst · ops.
  • Sustained an 85% open rate via weekly comms + monthly “Best CM Copilot Story” recognition.
